Profile

About Three-Zone

Three-Zone Sports Access is the member site for live high-school and youth games, schedules, and archives. Treasure verifies the member. Three-Zone authorizes playback. Every watch is recorded in the audit trail.

Signed-in members can also post photos and clips, upload a full game, trim a moment in Studio, and share media in Inbox. Feed ranking is chronological. It is not machine learning.

Support

Sign in with a member account to watch entitled live games and archives. Staff use Control plane at /ops.

  • Upcoming games stay listed until they go live. Watch live unlocks only while the game is live.
  • Archives play through the same rights check as live. If rights are held, playback stays off.
  • Search only returns schools, teams, and games you are authorized to see.

Privacy

Playback leases are HttpOnly cookies scoped to the media path. Search, live, schedule, and archive lists are server-filtered to your zone and package. Demo audit publication is labelled simulated until a production audit upstream is connected.

Terms

Access is entitlement-based. A live, archive, or full-game Watch action only plays when Three-Zone issues a lease. Public full-game uploads stay pending verification. You attest you have the right to upload media you publish.
