About

Three-Zone Sports Access is the member site for authorized live games, team schedules, and school archives. Treasure Network verifies membership. Three-Zone decides what you can watch.

Members see Live, Schedules, and Archives. Operators run events from the control plane.

Support

If a game will not play, it is not currently available with your access. Sign out and verify again if your session expired. Operators can restore rights from the control plane at /ops.

Privacy

Member sessions are stored as HTTP-only cookies. Playback uses a short-lived server lease. This site does not collect recruiting profiles or athlete identity beyond the team and game you are entitled to watch.

Terms

Access is limited to verified members with active entitlement and current rights. Live access does not automatically include archive access. Three-Zone may revoke a lease or event at any time.
